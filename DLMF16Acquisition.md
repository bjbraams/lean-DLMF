# DLMF Chapter 16 acquisition: Generalized Hypergeometric Functions and Meijer G

This acquisition covers all 101 numbered formulas and the analytical prose of
[DLMF Chapter 16](https://dlmf.nist.gov/16), version 1.2.8, checked on 2026-10-24.
Graphs, application commentary, numerical data, software listings and bibliographic
references without an explicit mathematical assertion are excluded. The mathematical
Askey–Gasper inequality 16.23.E1 is retained. The entry point is
[DLMF/GeneralizedHypergeometric.lean](DLMF/GeneralizedHypergeometric.lean).
New results remain in the existing `DLMF.Hypergeometric` namespace and reuse the
proved classical and regularized hypergeometric APIs. The initial acquisition introduced 165 direct
admissions; the second round proved 14 and the third proves six more, leaving
**145 direct admissions**. The latest proofs include finite inversions, odd Dixon,
normalized bilateral Dougall, the analytic raising operator and nominal ODE order.
Definitions and short completed proofs are distinguished from inherited admission
dependencies. [MathlibCoverage.lean](DLMF/GeneralizedHypergeometric/MathlibCoverage.lean)
records checked upstream matches, including partial scaled-series coverage.

## Domains and conventions

- Defining series, normalized principal analytic continuation and divergent formal
  series are distinct objects. Continuation existence for p=q+1 remains admitted.
  The guarded function definition selects an available continuation, otherwise
  retaining the native series. Local, entire and terminating series proofs,
  including `genHG_entire`, are now independent of that admission. Equivalence to
  the previous definition explicitly uses the retained existence theorem. The
  p>q+1 Barnes value has a separate contour/log-sheet interface.
- Conditional unit-circle convergence is natural-order convergence, not unordered
  `HasSum`. Terminating Gamma-pole cases use finite Pochhammer evaluations and
  whole-expression limits, including removable a=0 and c=1 values.
- Asymptotic powers use logarithm coordinates. Repeated residue poles are counted
  once, with the resulting logarithmic terms retained. ODE singularity predicates
  permit ordinary or removable degeneracies; nonresonant fundamental frames have
  explicit limiting adapters at resonances.
- The displaced-center formula 16.8.E9 uses the common principal component to
  correlate cuts. For16.10.E1, whose source specifies only appropriate conditions,
  both the formal coefficient identity and the analytic identity under explicit
  absolute expanded-sum hypotheses are acquired.
- Appell F1/F2/F3 use actual double series on their full bidiscs or diamond; F4
  reuses the existing local API. Continuations use correlated germs and Euler
  branches, with resonant connection limits stated separately.
- Meijer G retains ordered upper/lower blocks, the three separating contour
  geometries, logarithmic sheets, winding, integrability and contour independence.
  For16.19.E5 the printed positive-sign transformation needs n≥1; n=0 has the
  opposite sign and is acquired separately. The fundamental frame16.21.E2 has
  nonresonance hypotheses and explicit limiting frames when parameters collide.
- Existing proved Dixon, Dougall, Whipple and Appell F4 identities receive native
  Chapter 16 tags. Dougall's valid15.4.E25 tag is retained as well.
