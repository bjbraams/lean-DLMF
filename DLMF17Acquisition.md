# DLMF Chapter 17 acquisition: q-Hypergeometric and Related Functions

This acquisition covers all 186 numbered formulas and the analytical prose of
[DLMF Chapter 17](https://dlmf.nist.gov/17), version 1.2.8, checked on 2026-10-24.
Graphs, application notes, software listings, numerical data and bibliographic
references without an explicit mathematical assertion are excluded. The entry point is
[DLMF/QHypergeometric.lean](DLMF/QHypergeometric.lean), reusing `QSeries`,
`Gamma/QGamma` and `Bailey` instead of duplicating their q-Pochhammer, derivative,
Jackson-integral and Bailey-pair APIs. New generic qPhi/qPsi series preserve the
integer phase exponent from DLMF, including when the upper/lower lengths differ.
The numbered inventory below distinguishes definitions, checked upstream coverage,
proved adapters, admission-dependent proofs and direct admissions.

The second parallel proof round proves **28** statements, reducing this chapter
from 188 to **160 direct admissions**, including full-domain qPhi summability,
parameter limits, Gaussian limits and Jackson-series convergence. The third round
proves **13** more, leaving **147 direct admissions**: qPhi and infinite-product
holomorphy, six q-elementary expansions, two Gaussian bridges, and the analytic
product/germ continuation. All original parameter and convergence domains remain.

## Domains and source corrections

- Totalized defining sums are only identified with analytic formulas on their
  convergence regions. Continuations are separate analytic functions or germs;
  the TauCeti `ContinuesInside` interface transports a germ along paths inside
  a stated regular domain. It does not assign continuation values to a divergent tsum.
- Unilateral denominator regularity excludes the q-lattice; terminating identities
  require regularity only through their last retained term. Bilateral annuli include
  nonzero upper products and argument, with negative-index q-Pochhammer factors.
- The q→1 limit17.4.E4 is formal and coefficientwise: the bilateral annulus
  collapses, so it is not asserted as a pointwise limit of totalized bilateral sums.
  Very-well-poised definitions retain the source's degenerate a0=0 case.
- Jackson calculus has an explicit removable derivative at zero, finite-interval
  differences and two-sided bilateral summability. The Gaussian limit17.2.E34
  separates the fixed-lower-index case s=0 from growing s>0.
- The printed 17.7.E19 is false. The exact counterexample uses r=0,q=1/2,a=2,M=0.
  Its argument is corrected to a⁻¹q⁻ᴹ, following Gasper–Rahman, *Basic Hypergeometric
  Series*, Eq 1.9.7 ([primary reference, p.20](https://sci.uobasrah.edu.iq/images/Math/Basic_Hypergeometric_Series.pdf)).
- The printed 17.7.E23 is false. An exact rational counterexample is recorded in Lean.
  The corrected formula has denominator (p;p)ₖ and final factor (apᵏ/b;p)ₙ₊₁,
  following Gasper's 1995 lectures, Eq 4.1.23 after interchanging p and q
  ([primary reference, p.26](https://sites.math.northwestern.edu/~george/preprints/ggLecNotesq-Series/ggLecNotesq-Series.pdf)).
- The constant-term formula 17.14.E3 repeats the E2 left side although its right side
  contains G rather than H. Their linear coefficients differ. The acquired corrected
  left side uses (-q;q²)ₙ rather than (-q;q²)ₙ₊₁, as obtained from the displayed
  constant term. This correction is documented separately from the admitted identity.
- For17.8.E8 the actual bilateral series requires |c/b²|<1. The weaker printed
  |cq²/b²|<1 is handled through a separate continuation interface. Gamma quotients
  in 17.13.E3 have a removable-limit value at apparent poles; the regular strip has
  a literal quotient statement as well.
- q-Appell/Lauricella domains are explicit, including the empty-dimensional case.
  General Bailey transformation is proved by absolutely summable double-series
  interchange. q-Dyson constant terms use genuine Laurent polynomials, including
  n=0; analytic constant terms use normalized circle integrals with stated integrability.
